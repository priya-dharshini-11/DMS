About Dead Man's Switch Vault

Dead Man's Switch Vault is a secure digital system designed to store confidential information and release it to trusted nominees if the owner becomes inactive for a long period.

The project uses Flask, MySQL, Bootstrap, JavaScript, and Gmail SMTP to demonstrate authentication, role-based access, secure storage, and automated notification workflows.
